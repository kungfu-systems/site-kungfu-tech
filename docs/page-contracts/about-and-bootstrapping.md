# About and Bootstrapping Contract

The concise `/about/` route owns the public product, protocol, commercial, and
stewardship boundary.

`/about/bootstrapping/` is the deeper interpretive route. It argues that a
minimal human-sovereign core must first force the hidden organizational runtime
— memory, coordination, facts, permissions, and acceptance — into
machine-readable Work infrastructure before participation can scale.

The route must:

- distinguish that forcing function from ordinary software dogfood;
- explain why relative isolation matters;
- preserve the Session-to-Work and constrained-authority boundaries;
- record Kungfu's structural debt to Douglas Engelbart without presenting it
  as endorsement or direct succession; and
- invite an Agent to explain and challenge the argument, not simply accept it.

The essay may cite public Kungfu, KFD, Buildchain, and Engelbart sources. It
must not expose private coordination systems, internal paths, private Work
records, or unpublished operating detail.

Keep the maturity ladder explicit: design intent, merged mechanisms, qualified
evidence, released products, and independent adoption are different states.
