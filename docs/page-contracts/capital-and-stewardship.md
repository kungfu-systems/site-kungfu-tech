# Capital and Stewardship Contract

The Capital section has two reading layers:

- `/capital/` states stable principles for any future relationship with
  capital.
- `/capital/investor-perspective/` explains what could be underwritten, how a
  public protocol may support company value, which evidence would strengthen
  that thesis, and which risks and capabilities belong in investor fit.

The homepage remains product-first. Capital and Stewardship is a prominent but
secondary explanatory path, with stable discovery through About and the shared
footer.

Its homepage heading shares the preceding Builder cards' responsive title
scale and remains vertically centered against the full stewardship content.
Matching padding and transparent side alignment rails keep all three card
headings on the same left edge without adding visible borders.

## Stewardship Thesis

Keep three goals visible together:

1. global credibility for KFD;
2. commercial durability and expansion capacity for Kungfu Origin; and
3. long-term freedom of action for the project's author, stewards, and wider
   system.

The route must distinguish non-negotiable public-protocol boundaries from the
commercial and institutional capabilities a compatible capital partner could
add.

The value chain remains conditional: public KFD credibility can enable
independent adoption and a larger Agent supply-chain market, which can create
demand that Kungfu Origin may capture through products, enterprise delivery,
support, execution, and reputation. Do not claim that independent Hubs, broad
adoption, or the resulting demand already exist. Do not present KFD control,
exclusivity, or customer lock-in as a source of return.

## Public Claim Boundary

The pages are not a financing announcement or securities offer. They must not
publish an amount, valuation, pricing, allocation, timetable, subscription
mechanics, or transaction terms.

Statements about entity structure, intellectual-property ownership,
trademarks, protocol governance, investor rights, or a specific transaction
require separate verification and professional review.

The investor route includes a cross-border disclosure concerning the founder's
citizenship and Kungfu Origin Technology Limited's Hong Kong incorporation. It
may describe potential diligence, regulatory, institutional-policy, and
geopolitical friction, but must not classify a transaction as permitted,
prohibited, restricted, or notifiable. A real transaction requires current,
transaction-specific legal, regulatory, tax, and national-security review.
Official public links provide diligence context only.

Reject concealment, sham relocation, nationality-only conclusions, and
geopolitical arguments for taking control of the public protocol.
