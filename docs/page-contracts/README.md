These documents record the site-owned reading sequence, composition rules, and
public claim boundaries for each major product surface. They are reviewable
contracts, not substitutes for upstream product facts.

## Shared Rules

- The site owns reader progression, composition, navigation, and responsive
  presentation.
- Upstream packages own protocol, publication, qualification, maturity, and
  release facts.
- Human routes and machine-readable companions must preserve the same meaning
  and non-claims.
- A demonstration is not provider, durability, release, certification, or
  adoption proof unless its exact source contract says so.
- Human-facing KFD names use uppercase. Stable machine identifiers may retain
  lowercase layer IDs.
- Design intent, merged mechanisms, qualified evidence, released products, and
  independent adoption remain distinct maturity states.
